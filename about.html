Why we made it

Numbers should
support you.

Calories are useful information, but they are not a score. We built Calory to give you context without the noise — a simple daily estimate that makes your next choice feel a little clearer.

Use it as a baseline, notice how you feel, and adjust with curiosity. There is no perfect number and no one-size-fits-all plan.

What is the Mifflin–St Jeor equation?

It estimates your basal metabolic rate — the energy your body uses at rest — then pairs it with your daily movement to estimate total needs.
