Building a more
satisfying plate.

A simple framework for meals that carry you through the day — without making lunch another thing to optimize.

Calory editorial team15 May 2024
+
02

The best meal plan is one you can come back to. Satisfaction comes from a mix of nourishment, flavor, texture, and enough food to meet the moment you are actually in.

Instead of searching for a perfect plate, start with a few dependable anchors. They make everyday decisions easier while leaving plenty of room for your preferences.

Think in building blocks

Include a source of protein, something colorful, a carbohydrate you enjoy, and a little fat for flavor and staying power. The exact ingredients can change from day to day; the structure does not need to.

A balanced plate is less about rules and more about giving your future self something to work with.

Make it easy to repeat

Keep a few favorite combinations within reach. Cook extra grains. Wash the fruit when you bring it home. Small bits of preparation turn a good intention into a meal you can actually make on a busy Tuesday.
